Exercise 2.1: Safety Vocabulary:

Loss: An unacceptable outcome involving harm to people, property, or the environment.  
Example: A collision with a pedestrian causing injury.

Hazard: A system state that, under worst-case environmental conditions, can lead to a loss.  
Example: The vehicle is moving at high speed while a pedestrian is in its direct path.

Risk: The combination of the likelihood of a hazard occurring and the severity of the resulting loss.  
Example: A "High Risk" scenario is a vehicle driving at 50 km/h in a crowded school zone with faulty brakes.

Exercise 2.2: ODD Specification:

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dimension     | Operating Conditions        | Non-Operating Conditions                 | Runtime Detection
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Weather       | Clear, light rain           | Heavy snow, thick fog                    | Confidence scores from perception; raindrop detection on lens
Lighting      | Daylight, well-lit tunnels  | Pitch black (unlit roads), extreme glare | Ambient light sensor; image histogram analysis (underexposure)
Camera        | Clean lens, calibrated      | Obscured (mud/ice), misaligned           | Blindness detection algorithms (blocked pixels)
Scene Type    | Urban streets, marked lanes | Off-road, unmarked construction zones    | Lane detection failure; GPS/Map mismatches
Vehicle Speed | 0−50 km/h                   | >50 km/h                                 | Speedometer (CAN bus) feedback.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Exercise 2.3: Losses: 

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ID  | Loss Description                               | Why Unacceptable
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
L-1 | Death or injury to persons                     | Protection of human life is the primary safety constraint and a legal/ethical requirement 
L-2 | Damage to the ego-vehicle or external objects  | Leads to high financial costs, liability issues, and loss of mission capability
L-3 | Damage to the environment                      | Results in environmental harm or public utility disruption
L-4 | Loss of mission/transportation                 | The system fails to provide its intended utility to the user
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Exercise 2.4: Hazards:

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ID  | Hazard                                                                                             | Loss(es)       | Likelihood | Severity
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H-1 | Vehicle does not maintain a safe distance from other vehicles or obstacles                         | L-1, L-2       | Medium     | High      
H-2 | Vehicle enters a state where it violates traffic laws                                              | L-1, L-2       | Low        | High 
H-3 | Vehicle operates outside of its defined Operational Design Domain (ODD) without safe degradation   | L-1, L-2, L-3  | Medium     | High           
H-4 | Inadequate or confusing transition of control between the autonomous system and the human operator | L-1, L-2       | Medium     | High 
H-5 | Vehicle performs unintended or aggressive maneuvers                                                | L-2            | Low        | Medium
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Exercise 2.6: Unsafe Control Actions (UCAs): 

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ID     | Controller         | Control Action       | UCA Type           | Hazard(s)     | Unsafe Scenario
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UCA-1  | Planning Module    | Brake                | Not provided       | H-1           | The planner fails to issue a brake command when a pedestrian is detected in the path.
UCA-2  | Planning Module    | Accelerate           | Provided unsafely  | H-1, H-2      | The planner issues an acceleration command while a vehicle is stopped directly ahead.
UCA-3  | Planning Module    | Steer                | Wrong timing       | H-1, H-5      | The planner initiates a lane change maneuver too late to avoid a stationary obstacle.
UCA-4  | Human Operator     | Override/Takeover    | Not provided       | H-1, H-3      | The human fails to intervene when the system enters an ODD violation (e.g., heavy fog).
UCA-5  | Human Operator     | Deactivate Autonomy  | Provided unsafely  | H-5           | The human deactivates the system during a high-speed curve without taking steering control.
UCA-6  | Planning Module    | Brake                | Wrong duration     | H-5           | The planner applies full emergency braking but releases it before the vehicle has fully stopped.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
